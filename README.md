# IKEA Gifting – Customer Analytics & RFM Segmentation

Customer segmentation and churn-risk analysis for a UK-based online giftware and home décor retailer, built with RFM analysis and an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## Business Problem

IKEA Gifting is a UK-based online retailer specializing in giftware and home décor. The business wants to understand who its most valuable customers are and which customers are becoming inactive, so that it can plan retention and marketing actions.

## Objective

Identify valuable customer segments and understand churn risk using transaction data from December 2009 to December 2011.

## Business Questions

1. Who are the highest-value customers?
2. Which customers are becoming inactive?
3. What are the monthly and yearly sales trends?
4. Are there geographical differences in customer behaviour?
5. What marketing or retention actions should be considered for each segment?

## Dataset

- **Name:** Online Retail II
- **Source:** [UCI Machine Learning Repository](https://archive.ics.uci.edu/dataset/502/online+retail+ii)
- **Period:** December 2009 – December 2011
- **Size:** 1,067,371 records
- **Key fields:** Invoice, Product Description, Quantity, Unit Price, Customer ID, Country

The dataset is not stored in this repository because of its size. Please download it from the link above.

## RFM Methodology

| Metric | Definition |
|---|---|
| Recency | Number of days since the customer's last purchase |
| Frequency | Number of distinct purchase transactions |
| Monetary | Total revenue generated by the customer |

### Customer Segments

Customers are grouped into six segments: **Champions, Loyal, At Risk, New, Needs Attention, Lost.**

### Churn Definition

A customer is treated as **churn-risk** if they have not made a purchase in the trailing 90 days. This is a fixed inactivity threshold, not a predictive machine learning model.
